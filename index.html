Closed judges the last finished candle (final). Live judges the candle still forming — the same candle TradingView shows last — so it can change until that candle closes. Rule: the candle's close is beyond the 44 EMA with at least the minimum % of its body (|close − open|) on that side. Fresh = previous candle closed on the other side of the EMA. EMA slope = % change of the EMA over the last 5 candles. BTC & XAU always show their status (▲ above, ▼ below, – no signal); their candles use UTC days (00:00 UTC open), like TradingView. Gold uses Yahoo's XAUUSD feed, or COMEX gold futures (GC=F) if that is unavailable. Intraday timeframes cover Nifty 500 stocks; Daily and Weekly cover all NSE equities. Candles are anchored at 09:15 IST. Data: Yahoo Finance, updated by an automatic scan about every 5 minutes during market hours (GitHub can delay scheduled scans by a few minutes).
